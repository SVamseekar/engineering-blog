EU AI Assurance

How do you explain to a compliance officer that they cannot "edit" a model's training log to mark a failed safety check as "Exempt"?

To a legal auditor, a compliance assessment is a dynamic policy file—subject to update, interpretation, and retrospective categorization. To an engineer, a model's lineage is an immutable cryptographic log. When you force these two distinct mental models into the same tracking tool without architectural separation, you either corrupt your engineering ledger or invalidate your regulatory audits.

Inside the EU AI Assurance OS, we bridge this gap by combining CI release-gate contracts with assisted obligation determination. Using a grounded RAG over our control library, compliance officers receive assisted risk mapping and certification readiness scores—while human reviewers retain final sign-off authority before any model hits production.
