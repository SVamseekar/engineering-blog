MaSoVa Ops

Why do multi-unit restaurant managers still struggle with tablet pile-ups and lost delivery tickets during a Friday rush?

When in-house POS terminals and third-party delivery aggregators operate on isolated state engines, network hiccups and synchronous API calls create dangerous order discrepancies between the front of house and the kitchen.

Inside MaSoVa, our multi-tenant restaurant OS tackles this by combining an append-only local SQLite ledger with a unified order normalization pipeline. Inbound transactions write instantly to local storage—moving from RECEIVED to LOCALLY_QUEUED without blocking on cloud confirmation—while a centralized manager dashboard aggregates revenue, channel mix, and inventory in real time.

To assist with prep planning without risking automated errors, our demand forecast agent analyzes historical sales data to surface item-level prep suggestions strictly for manager review and approval.
